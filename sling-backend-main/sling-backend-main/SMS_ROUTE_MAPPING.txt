SMS ROUTE MAPPING - WEBHOOK TO SMS TEMPLATES
================================================

1. CARD ACTIVATION (₹0 - No Money)
   Route: customer_registered / ACCOUNT_CREATION
   SMS Template: 1207175640286853908
   Message: "Congratulations [Customer Name]! Your SlingNeo Prepaid Card - Issued by Transcorp ending [XXXX] has been activated. Happy Spending! - Team Payvoy India Private Limited"
   Variables: var=customerName, var2=cardEnding

2. POS TRANSACTION (₹[Amount] - Money Spent)
   Route: POS/ATM/ECOM (DEBIT transactions only)
   SMS Template: 1207175640002678858
   Message: "Rs.[Amount] spent on SlingNeo Prepaid Card - Issued by Transcorp at [Merchant]. Available Balance is Rs.[Balance]. Not You? Call on 7597182222 - Team Payvoy India Private Limited"
   Variables: var=amount, var2=merchantName, var3=balance
   Example: "Rs.500 spent on SlingNeo Prepaid Card at Amazon. Available Balance is Rs.2500. Not You? Call on 7597182222"

3. TRANSACTION REVERSAL (₹[Amount] - Money Returned)
   Route: POS_REVERSAL/ATM_REVERSAL/ECOM_REVERSAL
   SMS Template: 1207175640871398435
   Message: "Reversal of Rs.[Amount] is credited to SlingNeo Prepaid Card - Issued by Transcorp ending [XXXX] for unsuccessful transaction. Available Balance is Rs.[Balance]. Not You? Call on 7597182222 - Team Payvoy India Private Limited"
   Variables: var=amount, var2=cardEnding, var3=balance
   Example: "Reversal of Rs.500 is credited to SlingNeo Prepaid Card ending 1234. Available Balance is Rs.3000"

4. MONEY RECEIVED (₹[Amount] - Money Added)
   Route: M2C (Money to Card)
   SMS Template: 1207176939178335430
   Message: "Your SlingNeo Prepaid Card - Issued by Transcorp balance has been successfully topped up by Rs.[Amount]. Your available Balance is Rs.[Balance]. Not You? Call on 7597182222 - Team Payvoy India Private Limited"
   Variables: var=amount, var3=balance
   Example: "Your SlingNeo Prepaid Card balance has been successfully topped up by Rs.1000. Your available Balance is Rs.4000"

5. CARD UNBLOCKED (₹0 - No Money)
   Route: customer_cardstatus_update (unblock)

   Variables: var=cardEnding
   Example: "As per your request your Slingneo Prepaid Card-Transcorp Card ending 1234 has been Unblocked"

6. INVALID PIN (₹[Amount] - Transaction Failed)

   SMS Template: 1207175620327710861

   Example: "Your transaction of Rs.500 on your SlingNeo Prepaid Card 1234 was declined due to invalid PIN"

    Example: "Dear John, your SlingNeo Prepaid Card is closed. Refund of remaining amount requires verification"

================================================
MONEY INVOLVEMENT SUMMARY:
- ₹0 (No Money): Card activation, unblock, PIN attempts, registration OTP, card closure
- ₹[Amount] (Money Spent): POS transactions, transaction reversals, money received, invalid PIN, security failures, transaction OTP
- ₹[Balance] (Account Balance): Balance updates, closure reminders
